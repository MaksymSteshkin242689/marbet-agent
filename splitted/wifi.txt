WI-FI GUIDE

General Information:
- The Wi-Fi network name (SSID) for guests is: **Scenic Guest**
- The password for access is: **Spaceship1**
- Internet access is provided for free to all guests.
- Please follow the device-specific instructions below to ensure successful connection.
- Note: MAC address settings may affect connectivity, especially on modern smartphones. Be sure to follow all steps to avoid issues.

ANDROID SMARTPHONE

Step-by-step:
1. Open your device’s **Settings** and go to **Wi-Fi** or **WLAN** settings.
2. From the list of available networks, tap on **Scenic Guest**.
3. Enter the Wi-Fi password: **Spaceship1** and confirm.
4. Once connected, scroll to the **MAC address type** option.
5. Select **Phone MAC** instead of **Randomized MAC** to ensure consistent access.
6. You should now be successfully connected to the Wi-Fi network.

Troubleshooting:
- If the connection fails, try forgetting the network and repeating the steps.
- Make sure airplane mode is off and mobile data is not interfering with the connection.

APPLE iOS SMARTPHONE (iPhone)

Step-by-step:
1. Go to your iPhone’s **Settings** > **Wi-Fi**.
2. Select **Scenic Guest** from the list of available networks.
3. Enter the Wi-Fi password: **Spaceship1** and confirm.
4. Tap the small **blue "i" (info)** icon next to Scenic Guest.
5. Deactivate **Private Wi-Fi Address** (this ensures proper device identification).
6. Tap **Next**, then exit the settings screen.
7. You should now be connected to the Wi-Fi.

Troubleshooting:
- Forget and re-join the network if you encounter issues.

Support:
- For continued issues, visit the **Spa Reception** or contact Guest Services.
- Staff are happy to assist with Wi-Fi access on any device.

Security Tip:
- Always ensure you are connecting to the official **Scenic Guest** network.
- Avoid entering personal credentials or sensitive data on unsecured pages.
